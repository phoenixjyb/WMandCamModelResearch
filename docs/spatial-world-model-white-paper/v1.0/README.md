# White Paper v1.0 — Frozen baseline

**From Camera-Controlled Video to Spatial World Models**  
**从相机可控视频到空间世界模型**

V1.0 is the frozen first integrated Recomo white-paper stack for camera-aware video generation, spatial world models, World Labs Atlas, and filming-robot architecture.

## Scope

V1.0 established:

- a capability taxonomy from semantic camera language to calibrated and persistent spatial modeling;
- an evolution review from MotionCtrl and CameraCtrl through AC3D, RealCam-I2V, GEN3C, 4D correspondence, Rays as Pixels, and Atlas;
- the distinction between CogVideoX as a video backbone and AC3D as a camera-control adapter;
- the geometry-first Recomo architecture;
- a canonical camera and optics contract;
- separate camera, world, cinema, and robot/system evaluation;
- a functional-to-architectural counterpart roadmap.

## Frozen-version policy

This directory preserves the intellectual v1.0 position. V2.0 does not silently rewrite it. V2.0 adds the paragraph-level Atlas audit, `SpatialAnchor`, `SpatialContextPlan`, and the `ContextCompiler` under `../v2.0/`.
